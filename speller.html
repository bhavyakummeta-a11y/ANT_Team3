First 9 rounds train. The next 9 test unseen rounds. At least 7 correct selections are required to unlock experimental EEG control. Uncertain rounds count as incorrect.

What this demonstrates

The software maps a validated grid selection to text or a command proposal. It does not read arbitrary thoughts.

Previous movement recordings and the PhysioNet movement benchmark do not train this P300 decoder. Fresh highlight-aligned EEG is required.

Method & limitations

Three randomized passes of nine individual highlights. Features: 1–15 Hz, baseline-corrected 0–800 ms responses on available central/parietal channels. A regularized classifier scores attended versus unattended highlights.

Browser frame times are mapped to the bridge’s LSL clock. Physical display latency is unmeasured. This small, same-session test is an experimental gate, not a reliability certification.

Download contains stimulus markers, ERP features and evaluation, not continuous raw EEG. Record raw EEG separately with LabRecorder. Reconnecting clears calibration.
