Decoder concept

A production implementation could use filtering, feature extraction, calibration, confidence gating, and a cooldown to reduce accidental commands. In these recordings, physical and imagined conditions have not yet been reliably classified.

Do not interpret synthetic signal features as evidence of cognitive state, intent, or medical condition.
